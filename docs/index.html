Video Compressor for Android

Compress your video. Keep the quality you need.

Vidsize makes video files smaller directly on your phone. Pick a video, choose Balanced, Smaller or Smallest, and get a compressed file saved straight to your gallery. No account, no upload, no watermark.

How it works

  • On your device. Compression runs on your phone's own video hardware. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
  • Real numbers. You see an estimate before you start and the true before/after size when it finishes.
  • Saved automatically. The compressed file lands in Movies/Vidsize in your gallery — there is nothing extra to download.
  • No watermark, ever. On any compression level.
