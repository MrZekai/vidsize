Choose a size for sharing

How to compress a video for email on Android

  1. Select your video in Vidsize.
  2. Choose target size and select 25 MB.
  3. Review the estimate and compress.
  4. Check the final size before attaching it; email limits vary.

How to compress a video to 10 MB on Android

  1. Select the video you want to reduce.
  2. Choose target size and select 10 MB.
  3. Review the plan and start compression.
  4. Check the finished file: the target is not guaranteed.

Is Vidsize free?

Vidsize is free and supported by ads, with no subscription or in-app purchases. You can export with a small Vidsize mark, or watch an available rewarded ad for one export without the mark.

Rewarded ads require a connection and may not always be available.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Vidsize work offline?

Video compression works offline. Ads and the optional rewarded ad need an internet connection.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Vidsize processes the video on your device and does not upload it.

Is my original video deleted?

No. Vidsize creates a separate output and leaves the original in place.

What format is the output?

Vidsize saves an MP4 file with H.264 video and, when present, AAC audio.

Can Vidsize compress 4K video?

Vidsize attempts 4K sources using decoders available on your device. Compatibility varies, and output can start at 1080p or lower.

Why is the output size an estimate?

Encoding depends on the source video and your device. Vidsize attempts to reach a target, but the final size can differ or exceed it.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, German, Hindi and Arabic.
