How to compress a video for email on Android

A video can be too large to attach to an email. Vidsize lets you choose a target size and compress the video on your Android device, then check the result before you send it.

By BANGI · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Before you start: Check the attachment limit of your email provider. Some services count the entire message or encode attachments differently, so leave room below the limit. A 25 MB target is an example, not a guarantee that an email will accept the file.

Compress a video for email in five steps

  1. Select a video. Open Vidsize and choose a video from your device, or share a video to Vidsize from another app.
  2. Choose a target size. On the compression screen, select Target size. Choose a preset such as 25 MB or 10 MB, or enter your own target in MB. Pick a target below your email provider's limit.
  3. Review the plan. Vidsize reads the video and shows an estimated output. If it says the selected target is already larger than the source or too small for that video, choose a different target.
  4. Choose an output and compress. Continue with a small Vidsize mark, or watch an available rewarded ad for one export without the mark. Video compression runs on your device; the rewarded ad needs an internet connection.
  5. Check the finished file. Vidsize shows the result and saves an MP4 copy to your gallery. Check the actual size before you attach or share it through your email app. The original video stays in place.

Why the final size can differ from the estimate

File size depends on the video, its duration, and how the device encodes it. Vidsize estimates an output before compression and can make up to three encoding attempts when aiming for a target. It tries to stay under the target, but the estimate and final size may differ, and the target is not guaranteed.

If the finished file is still too large for your email, check the exact attachment limit and try a smaller target if the app says it is achievable. If a very small target is not reachable for your video, try a larger target, select a shorter video, or use another sharing method. Always check the output before sending.

What happens to video quality?

Reducing file size can reduce picture or sound quality. A smaller target may require more compression or a lower output resolution. Watch the result before sharing and keep the original until you are satisfied with the copy.

Does the video leave my device?

Vidsize compresses videos on your device without uploading them to a server, and you do not need an account. Compression works offline. When you choose to share a finished file through an email or another app, that app handles the sending according to its own settings and policies.

Quick answers

Can I aim for 10 MB instead?

Yes. Select the 10 MB preset or enter another available custom target. Some targets may be unreachable, and the final file can still exceed the target.

What format does Vidsize save?

It saves a separate MP4 with H.264 video and, when present, AAC audio.

Is the original deleted?

No. Vidsize writes a separate compressed copy and leaves your original video in place.
